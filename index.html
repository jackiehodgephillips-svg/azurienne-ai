What the Scorecard reveals

Three levers that separate high-performing yacht ops from the rest

Azurienne AI helps owners, charter managers, and brokers tighten the systems that move revenue, guest experience, and crew execution — then shows you where AI can help first.

01

Charter utilization

Empty weeks, slow responses, and fragmented inquiries leave money on the dock. Measure how reliably you convert interest into booked weeks — and where response time and pricing discipline slip.

  • Inquiry-to-booking conversion
  • Days vacant in peak season
  • Quote turnaround speed
02

Crew & guest operations

Provisioning gaps, handover chaos, and inconsistent guest prefs kill the “effortless luxury” promise. Score how cleanly your crew and guest journey run from first brief to last day.

  • Preference capture & reuse
  • Handover / checklist completeness
  • Guest NPS / repeat intent
03

Revenue leakage

Missed upsells, opaque costs, and broker misalignment quietly drain margin. Identify the silent leaks — APA surprises, ancillary revenue left unpitched, and deals that die in the handoff.

  • Ancillary / APA yield
  • Broker handoff integrity
  • Cost visibility by charter
