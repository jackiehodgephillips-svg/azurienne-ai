Best-practice questions

Rate each statement from 1 (rarely / not true) to 5 (consistently true for us).

1. Inquiry responses go out within a few hours — even on weekends in season.

2. We know our peak-season vacant days before the season starts — and have a plan for each gap.

3. Charter quotes are consistent, priced with clear logic, and rarely renegotiated from scratch.

10. We can see cost and margin by charter without reconstructing it from email and receipts.

11. Critical ops knowledge lives in shared systems — not only in WhatsApp chats or one crew member’s head.

12. We already use (or are actively piloting) modern tools / AI to draft replies, briefings, or guest plans.
